What We Typically Uncover

Real patterns we find in real businesses. You'll likely recognize more than one of these.

📞
Missed Calls After 5PM
Most inbound leads call during evenings and weekends. No one answers. No callback system exists. Lead goes to the next company on Google.
💬
No SMS Follow-Up
Lead fills out a web form. Gets an email auto-reply — maybe. Never gets a text. 78% of leads prefer SMS. Most businesses don't send one.

Slow Web Form Response
Forms submitted at 9PM get a response the next morning. By then the lead has already spoken to two competitors and made a decision.

Dead Leads Never Reactivated
Hundreds of past leads sitting in a spreadsheet or CRM — never followed up after the first attempt. A reactivation sequence could close 10–20% of them.
📅
No Automated Booking Reminders
Appointments booked — but no reminder sequence. No-show rates above 30%. Simple automation cuts that in half.
🔁
Manual Tasks Running on Human Memory
Staff spending 2–4 hours daily on tasks a $49/month tool could handle automatically. That's real salary being spent on robot work.
